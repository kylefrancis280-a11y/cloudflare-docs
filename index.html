Why Alignment Matters

WHY TRUCK ALIGNMENT
IS CRITICAL FOR YOUR FLEET

Every day your trucks run misaligned, you're burning money on fuel, destroying tires early, and risking lives. Ontario Trucking Association data shows up to 80% of Class 8 trucks operate misaligned right now.

01
UP TO 10% FUEL SAVINGS

Fuel Efficiency

Misaligned wheels create rolling resistance that forces your engine to work harder. For a truck covering 160,000 km annually, correct alignment saves $3,000–5,000 in fuel costs per year.

  • Reduced rolling resistance
  • Optimal tire contact patch
  • Less drivetrain strain
02
30–50% LONGER TIRE LIFE

Tire Longevity

Commercial truck tires cost $400–800 each. Misalignment destroys them prematurely — forcing early replacement of full sets costing $6,000–12,000 per vehicle.

  • Even tread wear pattern
  • Extended service life
  • Reduced blowout risk
03
CRITICAL SAFETY

Safety & Control

Poor alignment causes vehicle pull, uneven braking, and reduced stability. At highway speeds with 80,000 lbs loaded, this creates serious liability exposure and endangers lives.

  • Straight-line tracking
  • Even braking force
  • Predictable handling
04
$2,000+ COMPONENT SAVINGS

Component Protection

Misalignment stresses steering, suspension, and drivetrain. Prevent costly repairs to kingpins, tie rods, wheel bearings, and steering gear boxes before they fail on the road.

  • Reduced component wear
  • Longer service intervals
  • Fewer roadside breakdowns

The Real Cost of Running Misaligned

A single truck with poor alignment costs your operation $8,000–15,000 annually in excess fuel, premature tire replacement, and component repairs. Multiply that across your fleet.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How much does mobile truck alignment cost in Mississauga?
Scotia Tire Mobile Alignment charges $270 for a full 3-axle alignment (plus any required parts) and $95 for trailer axle alignment. All mobile dispatches include a flat $110 service call fee. Walk-in customers at 5647 McAdam Rd, Mississauga do not pay a service call fee.
Do you come to our fleet yard for alignment?
Yes. Our mobile unit is equipped with a Hunter 3D alignment system and deploys to your fleet yard anywhere in the Greater Toronto Area — Mississauga, Toronto, Brampton, Vaughan, Oakville, Burlington, Hamilton, Markham, and surrounding areas. We handle fleets from 1 to 100+ vehicles on-site.
What are your service hours?
Regular service hours are Monday to Friday, 5:00 PM – 7:00 AM. Emergency roadside service is available 24/7. Call (905) 670-1771 any time for immediate assistance.
What vehicles do you align?
We align all commercial vehicles including semi trucks / 18-wheelers, straight trucks, box trucks, buses, coaches, tanker trucks, flatbeds, dump trucks, and trailers. Our Hunter 3D system handles all Class 6, 7, and 8 vehicles.
Are you a certified Michelin dealer?
Yes. Scotia Tire Mobile Alignment is an official Michelin Commercial Service Network member and authorized BFGoodrich dealer. We've served the GTA commercial tire and alignment market since 1992, with tire expertise dating to 1945.
