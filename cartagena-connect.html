About Us

Direct Access to the Best of Cartagena

Cartagena Connect is your direct access to the best of Cartagena — without the guesswork, inflated prices, or unreliable bookings.

We specialize in connecting travelers with top-tier villas, private boats, curated experiences, restaurant reservations, and exclusive nightlife, all vetted through local relationships and real-time availability. Whether you're planning a birthday, bachelor/bachelorette trip, or a high-end getaway, we handle the details so you don't have to.

What makes us different is simple: we don't list everything — we send you only what's worth booking.

Behind Cartagena Connect is a team with over a decade of experience in hospitality, nightlife, and high-end event production. We understand what clients actually want: great locations, smooth logistics, and a seamless experience from arrival to departure.

Instead of spending hours searching through listings, you tell us your dates, group size, and budget — we deliver the best options and take care of the rest.
