### Email 2 — Day 1: The Atlas Score explained

**Subject:** How the Atlas Score actually works (no black box)
**Preview:** Five factors, one number, no spin.

---

Yesterday we mentioned the Atlas Score. Here's exactly how it's calculated — because you should know what you're looking at.

**Factor 1 — Momentum (30% weight)**
Where is the price relative to its day range? Is it up or down? A stock that's +4% and in the top quartile of its day range scores much higher here than one that's technically "up" but sitting near its low.

**Factor 2 — Analyst Breadth (20%)**
How many analysts cover it and what's the split? Five Buys and zero Sells is different from five Buys and four Sells with two Holds. We look at the whole picture.

**Factor 3 — News Momentum (15%)**
How many headlines in the last 7 days and are they positive, negative, or neutral? Recent news that's positive skews the Score up. A flurry of negative headlines drags it down.

**Factor 4 — Macro Alignment (20%)**
This is the one most platforms skip. Each sector is weighted differently depending on the macro regime. When the yield curve inverts, mining and defense score differently than tech and media. We model this explicitly.

**Factor 5 — Valuation (15%)**
A basic P/E percentile signal. Sub-12x trades get a boost. 50x+ names get penalised. Simple, but it's a guardrail against narrative-only calls.

Then Claude reads the same data and writes the narrative. The Score and the narrative are built from identical inputs — they always agree.

### Email 3 — Day 3: Your first sector deep dive

**Subject:** Which sector should you watch first?
**Preview:** Not all sectors trade the same way. Here's the playbook.

---

Atlas covers eight sectors. They don't behave identically — here's the quick cheat sheet.

**Mining** — Commodity-driven. Watch gold/silver macro, CAD exposure. TSX-heavy. Tends to pop on commodity news more than earnings.

**AI** — High beta, sentiment-driven. VIX above 22 compresses multiples fast. News momentum matters more here than almost anywhere else.

**Tech** — Rate-sensitive. Yield curve inversion + high fed funds = headwind. Best Atlas scores in tech tend to cluster when macro is easing.

**Biotech** — Catalyst-dependent. Atlas flags momentum but catalysts (trial readouts, FDA dates) aren't always in the news feed in advance. Use the Score as context, not as the whole thesis.

**Energy** — Fed funds and oil price regime. High rates can be a tailwind here if driven by growth. Sector macro alignment matters.

**Defense** — Counter-cyclical in stressed regimes. When VIX > 22 or the curve inverts, defense macro alignment scores jump. Good hedge sector.

**Media** — Rate-sensitive, structurally pressured. Hardest sector for pure momentum plays. Atlas scores are most useful here for avoiding the obvious traps.

**Other** — Everything that doesn't fit neatly. Worth scanning for outliers.

### Email 2 — Day 7: How to read the research note

**Subject:** Getting more out of the daily research notes
**Preview:** Bulls and bears aren't just labels.

---

The daily research note has three parts that work together.

**Bulls (3 factors)**
These are specific, numbered, dated. "5 of 7 covering analysts upgraded in the last 30 days" is a bull factor. "Strong chart" is not something we write.

**Bears (2 factors)**
Same standard. If there are no real bear factors, the Score reflects that — we don't manufacture risk warnings for balance.

**Insight (1 sentence)**
This is Claude's synthesis. It's the single most actionable line in the note. We prompt for it explicitly.

The best way to use these: read the insight first, then check whether the bulls and bears support or complicate it.
