## Script 4: Founder Story (2 minutes — long-form)

**Target:** YouTube channel, About page video, investor pitches
**Tone:** First person, honest, not polished pitch voice.
**Speaker:** On camera. No teleprompter feel.

---

[0:00–0:20]
"We didn't build Atlas because we thought we could beat the market. We built it because the research tools available to retail investors are genuinely bad.

Not bad as in inaccurate. Bad as in: they were designed for a different kind of investor than us."

---

[0:20–0:45]
"Most platforms give you price charts and some analyst ratings and call it research. What they don't give you is context. When the yield curve inverts, that means something different for a mining stock than it means for a software company. Most tools don't know the difference. They just give you the number."

---

[0:45–1:10]
"So we built the Atlas Score. Five factors. Momentum, analyst breadth, news sentiment, macro alignment — which is the one we care most about — and a valuation signal. Every factor is weighted. Every factor is derived from real data. Nothing is generated by inference."

[1:10–1:30]
"Then we use Claude — the AI — to read the same data and write a research note. The important part: Claude doesn't invent the numbers. It interprets them. Bulls, bears, one insight. Strict output format. No hallucination risk in the factors because the factors are computed before the AI ever runs."

---

[1:30–1:50]
"This runs every night. By nine-fifteen Eastern, you have scores for everything in our universe. By nine-thirty, the top movers across eight sectors have full research notes. You read them. You decide. We're not telling you what to buy."
