Marketing Plan

A four-week aggressive ramp on $3,000/month total spend, designed for scaling — not validation. Goal: 18–35 paying members in the first 30 days, plus enough creative volume to identify two evergreen winners that carry into month two.

Total monthly spend: $3,000 CAD Time horizon: 30 days Use only after offer converts at < $180 CAC
Don't start here unless the $50/day plan validated $100/day works because you skip the offer-validation phase. If you haven't proven the offer converts at < $180 CAC at the lower budget, scaling to $3K just burns capital faster. Run the $50/day plan first or run the first two weeks of this plan as a validation phase with kill criteria below.

Week-by-week ramp

Week 1 — Validate the scale ($700)

  • Meta: $60/day = $420. Three ad sets: cold lookalike-1%, cold interest-stack, and broad (Advantage+). 12 creatives across three hook angles. Optimize for landing-page-view in first 3 days, switch to "subscribe" event after $200 spent and event volume confirmed.
  • Google: $30/day = $210. Two campaigns: Search (exact + phrase match) and one Performance Max with audience signals (lookalike-style based on subscribers email list).
  • YouTube: $10/day = $70. Skippable in-stream pre-roll, one 30-second video (script #4 from Video Scripts doc), targeting Stockcharts.com, Investing.com, finance YouTube channels (TopTraders, Brian Tycangco) as placement.
  • Goal: 5–8 subs by day 7, blended CPC under $4, identify top 3 of 12 creatives.

Week 2 — Layer retargeting ($700)

  • Meta: $55/day = $385. Kill bottom 6 creatives, keep top 4, add 4 retargeting variations of the lead magnet (sample report). Retargeting ad set: $10/day floor, all visitors past 30 days.
  • Google: $30/day = $210. Add Display retargeting (visitors past 30 days, $5/day). Search keeps ramping with negative-keyword cleanup.
  • YouTube: $13/day = $91. Add a 15s bumper alongside the 30s — same hook, condensed.
  • Email: $14. Build the 7-email post-capture drip. Send first weekly market briefing.
  • Goal: 12–16 cumulative subs. Email list at 250+. Two evergreen creative winners identified.

Week 3 — Scale winners ($800)

  • Meta: $60/day = $420. Push 70% of budget to the top creative+audience combo. New ad set: lookalike-1% on email list of capture-only (not paying) — these convert at 2–3x cold lookalikes.
  • Google: $35/day = $245. Add 5 long-tail keywords from search-term report ("AI stock research Canada", "weekly stock pick newsletter Canada"). Raise bids on the top-converting term.
  • YouTube: $15/day = $105. Add the comparison-style video (script #9 — "Why I cancelled my Bloomberg subscription").
  • Email: $30. Send a re-engagement blast to non-converters from week-1 captures.
  • Goal: 22–28 cumulative subs. CAC trending under $130.

Week 4 — Compound & report ($800)

  • Meta: $60/day = $420. Split top creative into 3 placement-specific cuts: feed (square), Reels (vertical), Stories (vertical short). Same hook, three crops.
  • Google: $35/day = $245. Performance Max gets full budget control if it's beating Search ROAS.
  • YouTube: $15/day = $105. Test a 60-second long-form against the 30s — finance audience tolerates length when content is meaty.
  • Email: $30. Send the "year-in-review" / proof email with hit-rate stats from Atlas Score.
  • Goal: 30+ cumulative subs. Document evergreen creatives, winning keyword, and 3-month CAC payback math.

What to focus on (different from $50 plan)

  1. Creative volume becomes the bottleneck. Plan to ship 24 creatives in month one, not 16. Pre-batch using AI Image Prompts and Shotbreak Video Scripts in a single production sprint.
  2. Retargeting must work. Half the gain over the $50 plan comes from retargeting visitors who didn't convert. If your pixel isn't firing or audiences are too small, you'll spend twice as much for the same outcome.
  3. YouTube is the long-game lever. Pre-roll feels expensive per sub but builds brand recall — week-4 retargeting shows 2–3× higher CTR on people who saw a YouTube ad first.
  4. Daily monitoring, weekly editing. Don't touch ad sets every day — algos need 48–72 hours of stable spend to learn. Daily = look at numbers. Weekly = make changes.
  5. Build the affiliate / referral pipe by week 3. $100/day creates enough subscriber volume to seed a referral program ("give a friend a month, get a month free"). This is cheap-to-zero CAC and starts compounding by month two.

Hiring & outsourcing thresholds

  • $1,500–$3,000/month spend — founder runs ads, contractor does creative ($300–500/month for 8 image variations + 4 video edits).
  • $3,000–$8,000/month spend — add fractional media buyer ($1,500–$2,500/month) to free founder for product work.
  • $8,000+/month spend — full media buyer + dedicated creative pipeline (in-house or agency).

This plan reflects industry benchmarks for Canadian retail-finance SaaS in 2026. Treat projections as a planning range. The conservative column is intentionally pessimistic — if you hit it, the funnel is broken, not the spend level.
