At $50/day there's not enough budget to be in five places at once. We over-index on Meta because it's the cheapest place to test creative variations fast, and we keep enough Google Search to capture high-intent queries (people typing "best Canadian stock research").

Why no TikTok or YouTube at this budget TikTok requires constant new video volume to learn the algo — uneconomical until you have 5+ winning hooks. YouTube pre-roll has a $5+ CPV minimum competitive in finance. Both go on the $100/day plan, not this one.

Week-by-week ramp

Week 1 — Test & learn ($350)

  • Meta: $30/day = $210. Single CBO campaign, two ad sets (one cold lookalike-1% off email list seed, one interest-stack: "TFSA + Investing.com + Globe and Mail readers"). Eight creatives total — four images, four short videos. Optimize for landing-page-view, not subscribe.
  • Google: $15/day = $105. One Search campaign, exact-match only: [atlas analysis stock], [ai stock research canada], [best canadian stock newsletter]. Daily budget cap. No Performance Max yet.
  • Email tools: $35. Sign up for ConvertKit / Beehiiv free tier; install Meta Pixel and GA4 if not already.
  • Goal: 1.5%+ CTR on Meta, $4 or under cost per landing page view, identify the top 2 of 8 creatives.

Week 2 — Double down ($350)

  • Meta: $30/day = $210. Kill the bottom 4 creatives. Add 4 new variations of the winning hook angle. Add a retargeting ad set (visitors past 14 days, $5/day floor) showing the lead magnet — Atlas Score Sample Report PDF.
  • Google: $15/day = $105. Add 3 broad-match keywords with negative-keyword sculpting. Turn on conversion tracking to "Subscribe" (Stripe webhook → GA event).
  • Email: $35. Build the 5-email welcome drip in ConvertKit (templates in the Email Sequences doc).
  • Goal: First 2–4 paying subscribers. Email list at 50–100.

Week 3 — Scale the winner ($400)

  • Meta: $35/day = $245. Push budget to the winning ad set. Test a third hook angle (the "Stop paying $24K/year for Bloomberg" comparison). Retargeting holds at $5/day.
  • Google: $18/day = $126. Add a Display retargeting campaign (only to past LP visitors, $3/day cap). Search keeps ramping.
  • Email: $30. First weekly market briefing email goes out. Subject-line testing.
  • Goal: 4–7 cumulative paying subs. Discover whether the unit economics work — CAC under $200.

Week 4 — Optimize & report ($400)

  • Meta: $35/day = $245. Keep top creatives. Add a Reels-placement ad set with vertical-only assets (use prompts from the AI Image Prompts doc).
  • Google: $18/day = $126. Lower CPC bids on losers, raise on the top-converting keyword. Add a sitelink for "Sample Research Report" pointing to the lead magnet.
  • Email: $30. Send a re-engagement email to all non-converters from week-1 captures.
  • Goal: Hit 8+ cumulative paying subs. Document everything for the month-2 plan: best creative, best keyword, average CAC.

The biggest unknown Landing-page conversion rate moves the answer more than any ad-platform variable. A 0.6% LP rate produces ~5 subs for the month; a 1.8% rate produces ~15. What the focus should be

  1. Offer first, ads second. Use the Grand Slam Offer copy from the Hormozi Playbook on the landing page. Don't optimize ad targeting before the offer converts at all.
  2. Lead magnet over direct CTA. Cold ad to the Sample Report PDF (gated by email) outperforms cold ad to subscribe by 3–4x in retail finance. Trust ladder beats trust leap.
  3. Creative volume. Eight creatives in week 1, kill four, replace with four. Hormozi: most ads die — your job is to find the one in twenty that lives.
  4. Email is the moat. An email list of 500 by month-end is worth more than the 10 subs you closed. Future months convert from this list at near-zero CAC.
  5. Day-30 retention is everything. If churn after day 30 exceeds 25% you have a product issue, not a marketing issue — pause spend, fix product, then resume.

Tools & setup checklist (one-time)

  • Meta Business Manager + Pixel installed, conversion API set up
  • Google Ads account + GA4 + conversion event for Stripe subscribe
  • ConvertKit / Beehiiv / MailerLite — pick one, free tier fine to 1,000 contacts
  • Stripe webhook → email tool (so subscribers auto-tag and exit the prospect drip)
  • UTM convention: ?utm_source=meta&utm_campaign=cold_q2&utm_content=creative_03
  • One spreadsheet: spend / clicks / captures / subs / MRR per channel per week

If you only have 4 hours a week to run this

  1. Monday 60 min — review last week's numbers, kill or scale ad sets, write next week's plan.
  2. Wednesday 90 min — produce two new creatives using prompts from the AI Image Prompts doc, upload, schedule.
  3. Friday 60 min — write the weekly market briefing email, send to list, monitor opens.
  4. Sunday 30 min — daily-spend sanity check, refresh the spreadsheet, close any tabs that distract you.

Everything else is automated. The bottleneck for a small team is creative — invest the freed-up hours there, not in dashboards.
