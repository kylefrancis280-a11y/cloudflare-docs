Ad Prompts

30+ drop-in prompts for Midjourney v6, DALL-E 3, and Stable Diffusion XL — organized by hook archetype and platform. Each prompt is paired with placement specs, on-image text overlay, and a tested CTA. Skip the prompt-engineering trial-and-error and ship creatives this week.

Tools: Midjourney · DALL-E 3 · SDXL Platforms: Meta · Google Display · LinkedIn Aspect ratios: 1:1 · 4:5 · 9:16 · 1.91:1

How to use this doc

  1. Pick a hook archetype that matches your offer angle (six are listed below).
  2. Copy the base prompt into Midjourney/DALL-E/SDXL.
  3. Append the placement modifier (square / vertical / banner) — it controls the aspect ratio.
  4. Generate 4 variations. Pick the strongest. Add the on-image text in Canva/Figma using the suggested overlay (we don't ask the AI to render text — most models still butcher it).
  5. Pair with the CTA copy and ship.
The ten-creative rule Hormozi: most creatives die. Plan to ship 10 creatives at launch, expect 7 to underperform, scale the 2–3 that work. Don't fall in love with any single image — fall in love with the test.

Brand visual system (use in every prompt)

Atlas Brand DNA

Color palette: deep navy (#06080f), royal blue (#4f8fff), violet purple (#a855f7), accent green (#00e088), accent red (#ff4466). Dark backgrounds preferred.
Mood: institutional, calm, technical, premium — not "WallStreetBets memes" or "crypto-bro neon".
Style nouns: Bloomberg Terminal, financial cinema (The Big Short), Apple keynote slides, glassmorphism, data visualization, candlestick charts, subtle grid mesh.
Avoid: stock-photo handshakes, suited men pointing at screens, dollar signs, gold bars, lambo imagery, AI-generated faces with weird hands.

Quality control checklist before any creative ships

  • No AI-generated text rendered inside the image (always overlay in Figma/Canva)
  • No stock-photo cliches: handshakes, suits-pointing-at-screens, gold bars
  • No "guaranteed returns" or "make $X from stocks" language (Meta & Google reject)
  • Disclaimer-friendly: avoid claims that imply personalized financial advice
  • Atlas wordmark only — no "Atlas Analysis financial advisors" (we are not advisors)
  • Dark mode primary; provide a light-mode variant for daytime feed performance
Meta & Google compliance reminder Both platforms classify Atlas as "financial services" content. Avoid: testimonials with specific dollar gains, "guaranteed", "best stock", "double your money". Use: "research", "analysis", "structured", "educational", "data-driven". Disclaimer in landing page footer that this is research, not advice.

Production sprint plan (one weekend, 30 creatives)

  1. Saturday morning (3 hrs): Generate 4 images per prompt × 6 archetypes × 3 ratios = 72 raw images.
  2. Saturday afternoon (2 hrs): Cull to top 30 in Lightroom / Photoshop. Color-grade for Atlas palette consistency.
  3. Sunday morning (3 hrs): Build text overlay templates in Figma — one master file with auto-layout swaps for headline / sub / CTA. Apply to all 30.
  4. Sunday afternoon (1 hr): Export at platform-specific resolutions, name with UTM-friendly conventions, upload to Meta + Google.

Total cost: weekend of one founder + ~$30 in AI generation credits if using Midjourney / DALL-E paid tiers.
